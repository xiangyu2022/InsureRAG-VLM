SYN-HO-3 Base Homeowners Policy

Section I - Property Coverages
We cover direct physical loss to the dwelling on the residence premises shown in the declarations, subject to the policy limits, deductible, exclusions, and conditions. Coverage C applies to personal property owned or used by an insured anywhere in the world, subject to special limits and exclusions.

Section I - Exclusions
Water Exclusion
We do not cover loss caused directly or indirectly by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, water below the surface of the ground, or water that backs up through sewers, drains, or sump systems. This exclusion applies whether the water event is driven by storm, mechanical failure, or public utility backup.

Mold Exclusion
We do not cover loss caused by mold, wet rot, dry rot, bacteria, or fungi unless the mold results from a covered water loss and the policy or endorsement gives back coverage.

Section II - Liability Coverages
Coverage E - Personal Liability applies when a claim is made or a suit is brought against an insured for damages because of bodily injury or property damage caused by an occurrence. Coverage F - Medical Payments to Others applies to necessary medical expenses incurred by others because of bodily injury caused by an accident.

Conditions
The insured must give prompt notice of loss, protect the property from further damage, prepare an inventory of damaged personal property, and cooperate with the insurer in the investigation of any claim.
