SYN-HO-WB Water Backup and Sump Overflow Endorsement

This endorsement changes the policy. Please read it carefully.

Coverage Added Back
For an additional premium, we cover direct physical loss to covered property caused by water or waterborne material that backs up through a sewer or drain or that overflows from a sump pump, sump pump well, or related equipment. This endorsement modifies the Water Exclusion in the base policy only to the extent stated here.

Limit and Deductible
The most we will pay for all loss under this endorsement in one policy period is the Water Backup Endorsement Limit shown in the declarations. The all peril deductible shown in the declarations applies unless a separate endorsement deductible is shown.

Not Covered
This endorsement does not cover flood, surface water, storm surge, water below the surface of the ground, or loss caused by water entering through foundations, walls, windows, doors, or roofs. This endorsement does not increase Coverage A, Coverage B, Coverage C, or Coverage D limits.
