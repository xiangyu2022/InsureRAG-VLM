SYN-RT-1 Base Renters Policy

Personal Property Coverage
We cover personal property owned or used by an insured while it is anywhere in the world. The most we will pay for personal property loss is the Coverage C limit shown in the declarations, subject to special limits, deductible, exclusions, and conditions.

Loss of Use Coverage
If a covered loss makes the residence premises unfit to live in, we cover necessary increase in living expenses so the household can maintain its normal standard of living, subject to the Coverage D limit shown in the declarations.

Business Property Limitation
We do not cover business property away from the residence premises for more than $500 unless scheduled or endorsed. We do not cover property used primarily for business data processing unless a schedule or endorsement gives back coverage.

Identity Theft Exclusion
We do not cover expenses arising out of identity theft, unauthorized use of financial accounts, credit monitoring, replacement documents, or fraud recovery services unless an identity theft endorsement is attached.
