SYN-RT-ID Identity Theft Expense Endorsement

This endorsement changes the policy. Please read it carefully.

Coverage Added
This identity theft endorsement reimburses reasonable expenses incurred by an insured as a direct result of identity theft first discovered during the policy period. Covered identity theft expenses include credit monitoring, replacement of government identification, certified mail, notary fees, and lost wages for time reasonably required to meet with financial institutions or law enforcement.

Limit
The most we will pay for all identity theft expense in one policy period is the Identity Theft Expense Endorsement Limit shown in the declarations.

No Liability Coverage
This endorsement does not pay debts, unauthorized charges, fines, penalties, or damages owed to another person. This endorsement does not increase Coverage C, Coverage D, Coverage E, or Coverage F limits.
