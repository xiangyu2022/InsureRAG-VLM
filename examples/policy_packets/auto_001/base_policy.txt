SYN-AU-1 Base Auto Policy

Liability Coverage
We will pay damages for bodily injury or property damage for which any insured becomes legally responsible because of an auto accident. The most we will pay is the applicable limit shown in the declarations.

Physical Damage Coverage
Collision coverage applies to direct and accidental loss to your covered auto caused by collision with another object or by overturn. Comprehensive coverage applies to direct and accidental loss to your covered auto other than collision, including theft, vandalism, fire, hail, flood, or contact with an animal.

Public or Livery Conveyance Exclusion
We do not provide liability, uninsured motorist, medical payments, comprehensive, or collision coverage while your covered auto is being used as a public or livery conveyance. This includes carrying persons or property for a fee. This exclusion does not apply to shared-expense car pools.

Loss Duties
After an accident or loss, the insured must promptly notify us, cooperate with the investigation, submit proof of loss when requested, and protect the covered auto from further damage.
