SYN-AU-RS Rideshare Coverage Endorsement

This endorsement changes the policy. Please read it carefully.

Limited Rideshare Coverage
This rideshare endorsement modifies the Public or Livery Conveyance Exclusion for the covered auto shown in the declarations. We provide liability, medical payments, uninsured motorist, comprehensive, and collision coverage while the driver is logged into a rideshare or transportation network platform and available to receive a ride request, but before accepting a passenger or delivery assignment.

No Coverage After Acceptance
Once the driver accepts a rideshare request, accepts a delivery request, is transporting a passenger, or is delivering property for a fee, coverage under this rideshare endorsement ends unless another coverage part specifically applies.

Deductibles and Limits
The collision deductible and comprehensive deductible shown in the declarations apply to physical damage losses covered by this endorsement. This endorsement does not increase the liability limits shown in the declarations.
